A note for pre-501(c)(3) charities

As a pre-501(c)(3), some steps are handled manually, and the nonprofit email programs from Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace aren’t available to you yet — those require full 501(c)(3) approval. Your website and domain follow the same steps above, and we’ll add charity email as soon as your 501(c)(3) status is in place.
